# Sources and credits

The Rillow SEO guides are written for Rillow.
Their facts follow Google Search Central's documentation, checked in October 2026, and the guides link to it where the details matter.
Google's documentation is licensed under CC BY 4.0, and its code samples under Apache 2.0.
The AI search guide also follows Bing's Webmaster Guidelines and the crawler pages of OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity, checked on 13 October 2026.
The content guide's figure on self-ranked lists comes from Lily Ray's study in Search Engine Land, ["Google AI Overviews cite self-serving listicles, but recommend competitors 69% of the time"](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-overviews-cite-self-serving-listicles-recommend-competitors-480573), 18 June 2026.

The writing guide's rules are Anthropic's humanize-writing skill from Claude's built-in skills, adapted for writing on a website: the site's saved voice comes first, and save_article checks the patterns code can find.

The structure and many of the working methods build on other open-source SEO skills:

- [ivangdavila/seo](https://clawhub.ai/ivangdavila/seo) on ClawHub, MIT-0: the audit order, the ranking-drop triage, prioritising by traffic at stake, counting problems per template, the timing table, and many of the traps.
  Its parts that Google's documentation now contradicts were updated.
- [iannuttall/seo](https://github.com/iannuttall/seo), Apache-2.0: ideas only, such as what not to claim from data, verifying every fix, and giving every finding an outcome.
- [semrush/skills](https://github.com/semrush/skills), Apache-2.0: ideas only, such as treating tool metrics as estimates without universal thresholds, and sampling AI answers.
- [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills), MIT: ideas only, such as crawler controls by purpose and AI search by engine.
- [every-app/open-seo](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo), MIT: ideas only, such as finding link prospects among sites that link to several competitors, and grouping the sources AI answers cite by kind.
- [AgriciDaniel/claude-seo](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-seo), MIT: the idea of grouping keywords by the top 10 results they share.
- [addyosmani/web-quality-skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/web-quality-skills), MIT: ideas for performance work.
- [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins), the seo-ai-visibility skill: ideas only, such as never inventing facts and reporting no AI visibility score.

No text was copied from the Apache-2.0 or MIT sources.
